Release entries should include the deployed source SHA and link to the corresponding validation record. Do not describe incomplete human/device acceptance as passed.

## 0.1.0 candidate — 2026-10-17

- Add deterministic wrestling simulation, legal AI, procedural 3D presentation and touch controls.
- Add 12 wrestler entries and weekly career, rivalries, titles, training, promos, contracts and saves.
- Add replay/scenario coverage, synthesized sound, reduced-motion camera and alternate singles rules.
- Add CI and certification-gated integrity/activation/rollback tools. Human/device review and public playable activation remain open.


## 0.2.0 candidate — 2026-10-17

- Replace primary capsule characters with three CC0-derived humanoid GLBs, shared 53-bone skeleton and 38 original wrestling clips.
- Add synchronized move pairs, bounded contact alignment, semantic animation, independent clone state, fallback, pause and reduced motion.
- Replace dynamic controls with stable verbs, directional grapple slots, analog stick/run, keyboard/gamepad adapters and device layout presets.
- Add deterministic early-input buffering, authoritative counter telegraphs, revised AI/pin/recovery pacing and diagnostic reports.
- Migrate saves to v2 with frozen v1/v2 fixtures and separate browser storage; version new replay traces and package local Three.js addons.
- Human/physical-device acceptance remains open under 0048; no broader career/match-type expansion.
