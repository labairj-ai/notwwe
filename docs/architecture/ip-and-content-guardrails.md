# IP and content guardrails

## Working title

`Not WWE` is an internal development codename. WWE is a third-party trademark. Before public distribution or monetization, select an original public title and obtain appropriate legal review for branding/trademark risk.

## Originality requirements

Do not copy or closely reproduce:

- WWE logos or branded show graphics;
- championship belt designs;
- current or historical superstar likenesses;
- distinctive tattoos;
- exact ring gear;
- entrance music or melodies;
- catchphrases;
- branded show names;
- exact gimmicks/personas;
- proprietary commentary/audio;
- copyrighted game UI/art/assets.

Broad professional-wrestling archetypes, common match rules and generic wrestling concepts can inform original design, but characters and presentation must have independent names, visuals, stories and assets.

## Asset provenance

Every external model, texture, animation, sound, font or code asset must have documented source, license, redistribution rights and any required attribution under `assets/`.

## Content review

Before public release, run an originality pass over roster names, appearance references, logos, title designs, move names, entrance concepts and marketing copy. Rename/remove anything that reads as a one-to-one substitute for a real performer or protected brand identity.
